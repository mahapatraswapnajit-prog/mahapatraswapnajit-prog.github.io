Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Summary

GyaanPe does not require an account, a name or a phone number to learn. We collect only what is needed to run the app and to process purchases.

What we collect

Learning progress such as completed lessons, quiz scores and badges, stored on the device and, for backup, against a random device identifier.

Unlock codes you enter, and the random device identifier each code is used on, so that one code works on up to 3 devices.

Payment information is collected and processed by Razorpay. We receive the payment ID, amount and status. We never receive or store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords.

AI tutor questions your child types are sent to our AI provider to generate an answer. Please do not enter personal information in the tutor.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not show personalised advertising to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Service providers

Razorpay (payments), Cloudflare (servers), Google Firebase (progress backup) and our AI provider process data on our behalf, only to provide the service.

Retention and deletion

Learning data stays on the device until the app data is cleared. Purchase records are kept as required by Indian tax law. To ask for deletion of any data we hold, email gyaanpe.support@gmail.com.
