Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Unused codes: full refund within 7 days

If your unlock code has not been entered in the app on any device, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of payment.

Used codes

Once a code has been entered in the app, the content is unlocked immediately, so the purchase cannot be refunded, except in the cases below.

Always refunded

You were charged twice for the same purchase.

The payment went through but you did not receive an unlock code, and we cannot give you a working code within 2 working days.

We permanently stop offering a plan while your period is still active (pro-rata refund for the unused time).

How to ask for a refund

Email gyaanpe.support@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 70081 12031 with your Razorpay Payment ID (starts with pay_) and the reason. We reply within one working day.

When you get the money

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. The time for it to show in your account depends on your bank. The unlock code is deactivated when the refund is issued.

Cancellation

There is nothing to cancel: plans do not renew automatically. Premium simply ends when its period is over.
